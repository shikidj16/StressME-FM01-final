# Methods

M- and ME-model states were rebuilt using the revised 2026-08-31 INCA MFA workbook and the audited expanded mapping. The fitted net-flux table (`ID`, `Equation`, `Value`) was used; goodness-of-fit and residual tables were excluded. R1 was mapped to ATP-dependent glucokinase HEX1 and coupled one-to-one to measured glucose uptake, R10/PYK was retained, and R23/ME1 was omitted because it was removed from the workbook. Experimental growth and betaxanthin pins, the established overflow-share constraints, and condition-specific nitrogen rules; ammonium exchange was closed in formamide. The sensitivity intervention used all five candidate exchanges retained at their configured default bounds. M-model states were solved by parsimonious FBA. ME-model states were solved directly at the pinned symbolic growth rate with quad-precision qMINOS. Every ME state was accepted only at `inform=0`, `ninf=0`, and `sinf=0`. Carbon was computed as exchange flux multiplied by molecular carbon count. The complete reaction-level decomposition of every plotted lumped carbon category is provided in `publication_data/carbon_balance/carbon_balance_reaction_breakdown.csv`.
